## Rules — capture (Ramble; `docs/capture.md`)

- **Always-confirm.** Engines emit `TaskIntent`s with raw `dateExpression`/`personReference`; the deterministic `IntentResolver` makes drafts (no lifecycle position) and **backfills every empty field** (importance, effort, owner, kind, due date). Nothing is gated on confidence at capture; the Confirm card is the ONE human-in-the-loop boundary; every edit there is a `Correction`. The single confidence-tiered exception is destructive inference — the duplicate merge (0.85 accept / 0.5 undecided), extended to existing pairs by `DuplicateSweep` (kill-don't-delete, reversible, judged by `-DuplicateSweepEval`, never a judgment call as loser). **The sweep's prefilter is the lexical floor alone; the embedding ranks, it never gates (2026-09-17, on GA device numbers).** History: the 0.82 `embeddingFloor` was calibrated on synthetic unit vectors (≈ cos 0.985) and on 2026-09-12 dropped 10/10 real duplicates, while the beta judge returned one false merge at 1.00 on the prerequisite near-miss ("passport photos") — the reason the floor was not loosened then. On GA the judge scored that pair 0.50 and every duplicate 0.90–1.00 (FALSE MERGE 0/20), and the per-pair printout showed the embedding cannot separate duplicates (sim 0.00–0.18) from near-misses (0.03–0.57) at any floor. With the lexical gate alone the sweep merged 5/10 real duplicates in PRODUCTION with FALSE MERGE 0 (was 0/10); a pair missing a vector still gates on its words. `-DuplicateSweepEval` still prints real `sim`/`lex` per pair; argue any floor from those, never from the synthetic table. **2026-09-17, iOS 27 GA on the phone:** the GA judge scored that prerequisite pair 0.50 and every duplicate 0.90–1.00 (FALSE MERGE 0/20, gap 0.40) — the blocker is lifted on this corpus; and the printout shows WHY the floor admits nothing: in the store's `1 − √(2 − 2cos)` unit real duplicates read sim 0.00–0.18 while near-misses read 0.03–0.57, so the embedding floor cannot separate them at any setting and only the judge can. Moving or replacing the prefilter is an owner call over those numbers, not yet made. Inferred due dates come from a short explicit table (`inferredDueDate`), are suppressed on any blocker phrase, carry a `dueReason`, and never feed importance. A bare clock time means TODAY (`clockTimePattern`, one shared regex; a day word absorbs the clock beside it). A pronoun is not a blocker (`namesSomething`); `isBlocked` is derived from the phrase; **a short wait that OPENS on a pronoun points at the previous draft** (`IntentResolver.resolvingAnaphoricWaits`, 2026-09-17 — "book flights after it comes through" now waits on "Renew my passport…" and commits as a real edge to the sibling, not an external wait called "it comes through"). A capture made only of filler words is nothing (`Segmentation.isBareFiller`, whole line since 2026-09-18 — "hmm ok so" had become a card); categories score by keyword HITS, table order breaks ties, and the kitchen is `Home`. **A comma part carrying its own DEADLINE is an outcome, unless it opens on a pronoun** (`Segmentation.isItemLike`, 2026-09-20): `splitCommaList` vetoes the whole split unless EVERY part is item-like (a verb, or ≤4 words), and one noun-initial part took two unambiguous ones down with it — "renew my passport, book flights after it comes through, and daycare forms are due friday" came back as ONE task titled "Renew my passport, book flights" with the other two outcomes inside a wait chip. Found by eye, on a store screenshot. The pronoun guard is what keeps the third arm honest: "call mom, she's back from the trip on friday" carries a day too and is one task with context. **Measured before it shipped** — against the 2026-09-20 baseline (segmentation 51/52 golden · 23/26 real · false-keep 0 · adversarial false-keep 2 · coverage 96%) the hard case now passes and every other number is unchanged; the corpus case's own labels were wrong on the first run and the eval said so. Any further loosening needs the same evidence: false-keep is the critical error and `email the landlord about the boiler and the leak` sits one heuristic away. **Two clauses that each carry their own day are two outcomes** (`Segmentation.datedOnBothSides`, 2026-09-17): the bare " and " splits "dentist on thursday and the vet on friday" even though the right side has no verb, unless the right side OPENS with a weekday (a day-list, one outcome the resolver fans out) or says nothing but its day; measured on GA these were 2 of the 3 utterances still escalating, the on-device boundary pass answered "one part" for both, and the deterministic read resolves them in 2 ms — correct-local-resolution 89% → 92%.
- **Voice-first: the sheet opens INTO listening** (`ComposerView.RamblePhase`: `.listening → .understanding → .confirm`; `.capture` is the typed escape hatch). The orb reflects reception, never interpretation — no live transcript (it mirrors invisibly into `text`). Silence auto-submits after a fixed 5s that arms only on transcript deltas; orb tap finishes now; an empty finish settles to the canvas. Any non-user drop of speech settles to `.capture` with the words; backgrounding never submits. Every keyboard raise is a deliberate decision. **Never show an intermediate AI interpretation as truth** — test-enforced.
- **The microphone is never hot without a surface, and a stop always stops it (2026-09-20).** `SpeechCaptureService.start()` suspends twice — on the permission prompt and on the first-run model DOWNLOAD — and neither honours cancellation, so a `stop()` during warm-up tore down an engine that was not running yet and the suspended call then came back, installed the tap and set `.listening` on a sheet the person had already left: a live mic with no orb and nothing on screen saying so, and "Getting the mic ready…" at two seconds invites exactly that tap. `startToken` is bumped by every `stop()` and re-checked after BOTH awaits; a start that lost gives the microphone straight back. **A region we do not ship is not a language we do not speak:** `resolveLocale(preferred:supported:installed:)` (pure, `SpeechLocaleTests`) falls back exact → installed sibling → any sibling, sorted, because matching the full BCP-47 identifier switched voice off permanently for `en-NG`/`en-PH`/`en-KE` under a sentence that was not even true. **A framework's error text never reaches the person:** `sentence(for:)` answers in the product's words (`NSError` conforms to `LocalizedError`, so the canvas used to print "com.apple.coreaudio.avfaudio error 561145187"), and `isRetryable` separates a failed download from a verdict about the phone, with Try again beside it instead of a Speak button greyed out for the life of the sheet.
- **A REVEALED INTERPRETATION IS FINAL** — enforced structurally in `Interpretation` (`propose` refuses once `.revealed`; DEBUG fingerprint). The user may always edit their own cards. Product-wide: never expose an AI intermediate as user truth.
- **Routing is device-first, escalate on evidence** (`AI/CaptureRoute.swift`, `AI/CaptureEscalation.swift`): `.local` (deterministic, p90 2ms) is the default; the capture transmits only with a named reason — `emptyRead` (a REAL line the read could not draft; a line the segmenter dropped whole — filler only, a lone character — reveals locally as nothing, 2026-09-18) · `bigDump` (≥400 chars/≥6 items, straight to Gemini) · `underSegmented` (unaccounted boundaries ≥2; verifier-only signals may count but never cut) · `unresolvedDetail` · `lowCoverage` · `conversation` (spoken only; the authority may return NOTHING). The verifier asks no model and can only escalate. **`AppBrain.triage` has NO default route** — every caller states it; composer-free callers use `CaptureFlow.route(for:posture:)`. Structure the user typed never touches the network. There is always a free tail (`captureNeverBlocks`). The FM on-device model is OUT of the capture chain (measurable as `-RambleEval`'s `on-device(direct)` arm and `-FMDiagnostics`); it is a CANDIDATE for Ramble primitives after the GA evaluation, decided by a human over `-FMPrimitives`.
- **The boundary pass** (`AI/OnDeviceSegmenter.swift`) is built and **OFF** (`isRoutingEnabled`): the model names verbatim anchors, the app cuts, `CaptureEscalation.reason` is re-asked and final; nothing invented, nothing lost, grounding total (one missing anchor rejects the artifact); reachable only on `underSegmented` and on the on-device posture (`CaptureFlow.Arm.boundaryPass`, `boundaryPassAvailable` a REQUIRED parameter); **a cut is a gain only above the local read's count** (`isGain`, 2026-09-17 — the GA model cut a 3-outcome utterance into 2 on one run in four and the validator accepted the merge; now `.noGain`, and `-FMPrimitives` scores a no-gain over an already-right local read as `stand-aside`, not as a missing artifact). `-FMPrimitives` reports REACHABLE · POSTURE · POTENTIAL cohorts, gates fixed for the campaign, and `Instrument.runStamp` on every report; it scores the CUT count against the intent count (never the resolver's draft count, which fans a day-list — that mis-scoring read the GA model's correct 8-part cut as a false accept on 2026-09-17) and prints the model's anchors on a FALSE-ACCEPT row. **GA verdict (2026-09-17, 24A437):** FALSE ACCEPT 0/0, precision 100%, p50 1.31 s / p95 2.46 s in envelope; HOLD only on REACHABLE recall (1/3 exact — two "one part" refusals).
- **The cloud arm is `FoundationModelsEngine` with a different `SessionSource`, not a second engine** — same instructions, schema, verbatim-quote grounding and resolver on both arms. `triage` records the rung ACTUALLY taken. Candidates ride the chain (retrieval concurrent with generation, bounded by `candidateWaitSeconds` 0.25s) — **a feature whose enabling input is an unset default parameter fails silently and tests green; assert threaded data at the point of use.** Capture-time model reasoning streams internally; the user receives ONE final interpretation. Provisional deterministic cards render first; `DraftMerge` Pass C survives for the re-submit path only.
- **Capture is the conservative stage**: understand what was said; never invent a task's existence. `ExtractedTask.sourceQuote` must be found in the raw capture (`AppBrain.grounded`) or the task is dropped, never substituted. **One task per distinct intended OUTCOME** — quantity/cadence never multiplies, named occasions do; the model hands the phrase over, `IntentResolver.expand` fans out (all-or-nothing, `maxInstances` 7). **Structure is decided ONCE at submit** (`Segmentation.structure(of:)`: `.explicit` or `.unstructured`). The deterministic title is a task, not a transcript (`cleanTitle`, meta-narration stripped, a cut-off "at" becomes the "When?" ask; **the day lifted into the chip leaves the title's EDGE** — `strippingEdgeDate`, 2026-09-17: "call the dentist thursday" → "Call the dentist" under a Thu chip, as the model arm already wrote it; a mid-line day is a modifier and stays, a clock time stays, a day after about/regarding/re is a TOPIC and stays; "the 3rd of October" is a date, and a bare month behind a preposition ("in July") is the first of its next occurrence (`IntentResolver.parseBareMonth`); a named SUBJECT — "Maya needs to…" — is the owner and leaves the title in the ENGINE, never the segmenter; a trailing JUSTIFICATION after a comma ("…, it's like 40 quid a month") leaves too (`strippingTrailingReason`); **the dependency clause leaves too** — `strippingTrailingWait`, 2026-09-18: "book flights for the trip after passport is done" → "Book flights for the trip", cut at the first block signal when at least two words precede it, so "look after the kids" keeps its words).
- **Budgets and hedges:** deadlines derive from `WaitContext` via `ModelDeadline.seconds(for:)`; capture's is sized by WHY the words left (`captureSeconds(for:)`: 30s for dumps/empty reads, 10s standby when a plausible read is in hand); a deadline hit on a long ramble is `.salvaged`, not a failure — chunk, never raise. The hedge is retired (`hedged(hedge: nil)`); a cancelled capture is not a timeout. **Capture does not consult `CloudBudget`.** The on-device confidence GATE was built, measured (2303ms, 95–100% escalation) and deleted — do not rebuild without new evidence. **Bracket every new metric before pointing it at a model** (the instruments lie more often than the code).
- **Privacy is per-workload and stated in plain words** (`Models/DataBoundary.swift`, no vendor names, test-pinned): Ramble's raw capture is the ONE sanctioned raw-text transmission (`transmitsRawCapture`), audio never leaves, **the Advisor sends structured snapshots and the CLOUD arm gets `.bare` facts — no notes, no capture quote, no reasoning, no learned preferences (fixed 2026-09-20)**; the cloud rung had been handed the facts whole, unclipped, while the same screen promised "never your raw notes", and `DataBoundary`'s own note says the asymmetry is what makes the capture sentence acceptable; `AdvisorCloudBoundaryTests` greps the prompt and the call site, and deliberately pins that the ON-DEVICE arm still sees everything, so nobody "fixes" this by starving every reading; **corrections and history never reach US and never reach anyone invited — NOT "never leave this device", which was false until 2026-09-20** (the private store carries `NSPersistentCloudKitContainerOptions` at `.private` scope with no entity exclusions, so every entity mirrors to the person's own iCloud; what the old sentence described was the `CKShare`, which those entities genuinely never ride). `neverSentence` and `syncSentence` split the two facts, `captureShort`'s quiet arm is scoped to "not sent anywhere to be understood" because onboarding shows it without the sync line beside it, and `CapturePostureTests` pins all of it. *A privacy sentence that is wrong in the person's favour is still wrong, and it sits on the screen where the app asks to be believed.* sentences for sync and telemetry appear only while each is true. **The privacy posture is GONE (2026-10-04):** capture reads on the device only, so there is nothing to choose — `CapturePosture`, its chip and its `DataBoundary` arm are deleted; `PrivateCaptureEngine` now runs for ONE thought the read could not land (`emptyRead`/`unresolvedDetail`) when a model is present, still grep-pinned against the cloud seam alongside `CaptureJudge` and `CaptureFlow`. **The composer holds ONE `PrivateCaptureEngine` for the sheet's life** (2026-09-17): prewarm on appear, `transcriptChanged` on every delta, `silenceArmed` when the window arms for a single thought, `finish` at submit, `cancel` on disappear — a fresh engine at submit reached none of that and every private capture paid the cold read; measured on the phone the wait after the window fell from p50 1308 ms to 5 ms (19/19 speculative), the receipt's `armWon` says which path served. **Ramble economics** (five rules, `docs/capture.md`): no visible quota; models produce artifacts, deterministic validators decide acceptance (false accept is the critical error); every cloud call metered from `Response.usage`; every report run-stamped, quality/latency gates and cost the optimizer; Remote Config / kill switches may turn a pillar's cloud arm off, never a floor.
- **The reveal shows the consequence, not the classifier** (`isJudgmentCall` only — never `draft.needsDecision`; `.planning` reads "Needs a plan"; uncertainty is not a visual state; the TARGETED ASK is `TaskDraft.unresolved` → "When?" chip, never on an undated task). The orb (`RambleOrb`) is a two-hue `MeshGradient` sphere, never a spinner — rate, not shape, decides it; audio controls presence never speed; both mode branches live in ONE `case .listening, .understanding:`; minimum dwell `orbMinimumDwellSeconds`; judge on VIDEO. **Create ends the loop immediately**; the count is unstated; only a merge speaks after (`UndoNotice`). The 2026-09-04 surface pass and the three composer layout traps are pinned in `CaptureSurfaceTests` and `docs/capture.md`; **the fourth (2026-09-18) is fixed width inside a vertical `ScrollView`, which CENTRES over-wide content** — the reveal's add-more row is glyph-only at accessibility sizes, the canvas title holds its height (`fixedSize`, subtitle hidden there), and `canvasCompressedFloor` is one line at the CURRENT text size, never the 44pt hit target. **Every surface pass includes `simctl ui … content_size accessibility-extra-large`** — five of the six defects of 2026-09-18 lived only there.
- **A defect found by eye becomes a corpus case, not an argument (2026-09-20).** A store screenshot exposed "renew my passport, book flights after it comes through, and daycare forms are due friday" reading as ONE draft titled "Renew my passport, book flights" with the other two outcomes inside a wait chip. Cause: `splitCommaList` requires EVERY comma part to be item-like (a verb, or ≤4 words) and vetoes the whole split when one is not — "daycare forms are due friday" opens on a noun, so the two unambiguous parts went down with it; same veto for "daycare forms are due friday and the oil change is overdue". The router DOES notice (`underSegmented`), so a cloud-reachable build reads it correctly and the free tail is what under-reads — which is the documented design, and exactly why the gap belongs in the measured number. It is now an `evalSet` case (the golden corpus, not the adversarial suite — that one is paired by construction and its own test rejects half a pair). **Do not loosen the veto without re-running `-RambleEval`:** false-keep is the critical error, `email the landlord about the boiler and the leak` sits one heuristic away from splitting, and the 2026-09-20 baseline to beat is segmentation 51/52 golden · 23/26 real · false-keep 0 · coverage 96%.
- **Eval floors live in the app target** (`AI/RambleEvalSet.swift`, `RambleEval.Floors`): CI and `-RambleEval` read the same numbers; the real-utterance corpus (`realSet`, `Floors.real`) is quarantined; every model arm proves it ANSWERED (served <90% = DEGRADED, naming the error); the cloud arm is opt-in (`-WithCloud`, quota-guard provider otherwise). Re-calibrate floors only downward from observed numbers.
- **The cloud rung is OFF — the product reads on the device only (2026-10-04, the owner's call: the product is free and cannot carry a per-call bill).** `CloudModel.isEnabled` is a constant `false` at the ONE gate (`isAvailable = isEnabled && provider.isAvailable`), so capture routing, the Advisor's rung choice and both privacy sentences all follow it, and `AppDelegate` never configures Firebase (no App Check handshake, no network behind the seam) — "off because a gitignored plist was absent" would have been reversed by any archive made on a machine that has the file. Baseline with zero cloud calls (`-RambleEval`, 2026-10-04): segmentation 52/53 golden · 23/26 real, every other column 100%, 96% of unstructured captures kept local, false-keep 0. **Not yet measured on-device-only, and the cost of removing the cloud:** a ≥400-character dump (`bigDump` went straight to the cloud) and chatty speech with no task in it (`conversation`: the cloud could answer NOTHING; the local reader makes junk cards). The fallback under design is one task from the whole text with a "Split into N?" offer, never a model arm that segments. `CloudOffTests` pins the gate and the guarded `configure()`. The provider, `CloudBudget`, `CloudHealth`, App Check and the kill switches stay until a removal pass deletes them together with the manifest's raw-words type and the policy's escalation paragraph. While the flag is off the App Check deadline (2026-11-02) and the App Attest capability are moot.
- **The boundary pass stays OFF, and the reason is now a number on the population it would serve (2026-10-04, `-DumpEval`).** With the cloud gone the on-device boundary pass is the obvious stand-in for the cloud's `underSegmented` answer, so it was measured on what the corpus had never held: twelve authored parent dumps (long dictation, school emails, run-ons, chatty speech; labels are Claude's stand-in for the parent-network captures, kept in `AI/DumpEval.swift` apart from every floor). **Deterministic read:** every expected outcome appears in some card (recall 12/12 — no intent lost) but the card COUNT is exact only 3/12 — fused cards on one side, non-task cards ("Dear parents, thank you…", "So I was thinking") on the other. **Boundary pass, real model, zero cloud:** accepted 3/12 and ALL THREE WERE WRONG — 10 cards for a 6-item list, 7 cards for a capture with no task in it, 4 cards for one task — and it timed out twice at the 3.5 s cap (p50 1.3 s). Its GA "FALSE ACCEPT 0/0" was measured on a corpus with no dumps; that evidence does not transfer. So: it stays off, the deterministic read stays the default, and no model arm segments. **What the run found instead:** the commonest way a parent opens a dictated list — "for thanksgiving I need to order the turkey, ask…, buy…" — fused six outcomes into two cards, because the first part opens on its OCCASION and `splitCommaList`'s all-or-nothing veto threw the whole list out. `isItemLike` has a fourth arm for exactly that shape (a short occasion · the person's own need-statement · a real action verb, nothing looser) and the case is in the golden corpus: golden 52/53 → 53/54, real unchanged at 23/26. **Still open:** non-task sentences become cards (an email's greeting and sign-off) and two run-ons still fuse their last two outcomes; each wants the same treatment — a deterministic rule, a corpus case, `-RambleEval` unchanged — never a model.
- **The read makes the pieces; the on-device model says what each piece is; the app decides what the verdict may do (`AI/CaptureJudge.swift`, 2026-10-04, the owner's redesign).** `CaptureFlow.plan(text:localRead:fromVoice:modelAvailable:)` has four arms and none transmits: `.judge` (a model + a doubtful piece), `.privateEngine` (a model + one thought the read could not land), `.revealAfterDwell` (spoken), `.revealInstantly` (typed). The composer no longer calls `AppBrain.triage`; `CaptureRoute`, `triage`'s cloud arm and the provider survive only for onboarding, the seams and the evals until the deletion pass. **The judge:** one call per doubtful piece (`PieceRead`: task / several / none), all at once, 4 s per call and 6 s for the pass, with a head start in the silence window. **What a verdict may do:** `none` SETS ASIDE — the piece rides the proposal as `Interpretation.leftOut`, shows under the cards with a one-tap way back, and the subtitle counts it; never applied to a piece that states a need (`statesANeed`), to a piece of ≤3 words, or to a one-piece capture. `several` licenses `resplit`, which the app validates: every part must `standsAlone` or the piece stays one card; a short imperative on "it" folds into the piece before (`foldingAnaphora`). No answer keeps the piece. **Measured (sim:iPhone18,1, 26A428):** per piece FALSE DROP 0/57 on the dumps and 0/108 on the Ramble corpora's task clauses, fused pieces caught 3/3, p50 ~0.5 s a call; end to end `-DumpEval` exact card count 4/12 → 8/12 with no expected outcome missing, Ramble piece count 76/80 → 76/80 (broke 0), whole read p50 0 ms / p95 1.3 s. One list call for the whole capture was 3× faster and made 1 false drop in 57 — do not switch to it. A wording change aimed at past-tense chatter moved nothing and was reverted. **Second pass (same day):** the judge's call also offers a to-do TITLE for a doubtful piece, accepted only by `validatedTodo` (their verb — said, or its -ing/-ed form, with get/buy only for a need for a thing; their instruction verb when they gave one; every name kept; no date; nothing vague); a short REPORT ("The pediatrician called") folds into the next card as `TaskDraft.context` (notes at Create) by a DETERMINISTIC rule — as a fourth model verdict, "background" lost 4/108 real tasks and was reverted; a card judged several that `resplit` refuses offers "Split into separate tasks", which asks `modelSplit` and accepts only parts that all pass the validator (4/6, the refusals correct); a long one-line remark may be set aside; answers that land before the pass budget are kept. **Onboarding reads through the same pipeline** (`CaptureJudge.readCapture`) with its own left-out section. Numbers: FALSE DROP 0/108 and 0 on the dumps, dumps exact 4/12 → 9/12 with nothing missing. **Still open:** phone latency (the numbers above are Mac silicon). `capture_judged{left_out, restored}` is the false-drop meter in production. Change the instructions or the validators only with `-CardJudgeEval` FALSE DROP 0 and `-DumpEval` not worse.
