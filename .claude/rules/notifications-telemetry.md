## Rules — notifications, metrics, telemetry

- **ONE notification: the Sunday household digest** (`Features/Digest/`), a new carve-out argued in `prev-docs/product-guardrails.md` (2026-09-12) whose conditions are code: nil on an empty week schedules nothing, one identifier, passive, no badge, on by default only for two caretakers, permission asked once when the household becomes shared, excluded from `selfInitiatedOpens`, suppressed while the app is open, killable never reworded. The daily nudge's 2026-09-02 closure stands; a second notification type is the abuse signal.
- **Required Attention is the master metric** (`Models/RequiredAttention.swift`): capture · orientation (re-owned by the day answer) · execution · maintenance fall, progression lift rises; nil means "not measurable" (`—`), local-only, DEBUG-surfaced, never a user-facing score — **the whole Settings Diagnostics card is `#if DEBUG`** (2026-09-18: its "Kept N% · rot N%" line had shipped to customers). `HouseholdActivation` joins it (activated/retained/single-caretaker, anchored on the ACCEPTED invitation).
- **User data is local-first; product telemetry is not** (`Models/Telemetry.swift`): `TelemetryEvent` is a closed enum whose every payload is an enum or bucket — **no `String`/`Int`/`Date`/`UUID` parameter, grep-pinned by `TelemetryAllowlistTests`**; `AI/StatsigSink.swift` is the only file importing the vendor (client key `Info.plist` `StatsigClientKey`, empty → no sink); opt-out checked before the vendor; anonymous install id reset with the store; **the group code (`TelemetryGroup`, 2026-10-04) is a salted SHA-256 of `Household.id` set as the Statsig custom id `groupID` on the sink's identity, never an event payload**, plus a `groupSize` bucket. `TelemetryGate` names kill switches only (`killCloudCapture`/`killCloudAdvisor`/`killWeeklyDigest`), failing closed as "not killed". Never a title, a name, a raw count or duration.
- **The Brief is CUT** (2026-09-02): two equal pillars, Ramble and the Advisor. "What deserves me today?" survives as a question the day answer (`HouseholdChatFloor` `.today`, `TodayQueries`) answers instantly; no Recap, no replay predicate, no `CapacityLog` input. Two precedents survive: the model-ranks reversal and the tier-inversion argument. `CapacityLog`/`CapacityBaseline`/`deferralCount`/`carriedOverCount` are inert; only the per-task judgment layer is ever called the Advisor.
- **The unit of measure is the group: one household of 1 or N** (`Models/GroupMetrics.swift`, 2026-10-04). A solo parent is a group of one, counted like any other; volumes are normalized per ACTIVE member on the dashboard, times are medians per group then across groups, and participation and load share are nil for a group of one. Linear's measures for a family: `task_completed{lead, cycle}` (age buckets), `capture_committed{triage}`, and one weekly `group_snapshot` per install (open count, stale/hand-off/participation/load as share buckets; the console keeps one per group per week). `HouseholdActivation` (both caretakers) stays as a participation reading and is no longer the activation metric.
