## Rules — surfaces (`docs/surfaces.md`)

- **Navigation: Ask is the home, Tasks is a SHEET behind its header button (2026-09-23, the owner's swap over the argument that a list answers a glance and a chat answers a question — `docs/decisions.md`)**: `RootTabView` shows `HouseholdChatView` at the root of one `NavigationStack`, opened ORIENTED on the day answer (never blank while anything is open; it re-seats on `facts.stableFingerprint` and never scrolls its openers); the list button presents `TasksSheet`, Manage Household pushes on whichever stack asked. The list was a pushed page for a few hours and Back from it faulted on the owner's phone — never a push. **`ShellSurfaces` is the one host for the composer, Activity, Settings, the commit receipt and the 62pt orb; mounted once at the root and once inside the Tasks sheet** (each with its own `ShellSurfaceController`), because a sheet cannot present over another sheet from the same presenter; every `\.openCapture`/`\.openActivity`/`\.openSettings`/`\.openRoster`/`\.openTasks` resolves to the context the view is in. **The capture orb is `CaptureOrbButton` in two placements, one rule**: 44/28pt inside the home's `ChatComposerBar` (`onCapture`, in the bar's row so it rides the keyboard with nothing to collide with), 62/40pt bottom-trailing over the list (hidden under the keyboard); both pause on `\.orbCovered`. Send/Stop live INSIDE the field and appear with the first character. **The home reads as a place** (design pass 2026-09-23): title "Ezra" with the date as `.navigationSubtitle` (no kicker in the thread), the glance strip WRAPS under a cap of six (never a scroller clipping a count) with HIG tap targets, and the list button is titled "Tasks", never a bare glyph. **A to-do typed into the question box is OFFERED back, never guessed** (`CaptureOffer.looksLikeCapture` → `ChatCaptureOffer`: Add it as a task · Ask it anyway; metered by `captureOffer(outcome:)`; reached by `-SendAsk "text"`). **The home is personal AND shared**: the second opener is `HouseholdCatchUp` — "Since you last looked, Maya finished “…”" over the change log (other members' human acts since `home.lastLookedAt`, written on background; a first look reaches back 7 days; 3 named then "and N more in Activity"; "handed you" leads), a sentence with rows, never a badge; the chips add "What deserves Maya today?" and the glance strip the other caretakers' loads by name (2 at most). **The swap is judged on two events** (`askAsked(scope, route)` on a person's question, `tasksOpened` on the button — never on a seam) against `captureStarted` per session. **A chat thread's ForEach reads its neighbours from its OWN snapshot, never the live store by index** (both chats, 2026-09-23): the home's thread SHRINKS when its openers re-seat (a clear, a sync, a lifecycle move on the list), and `store.messages[index - 1]` from an index over the old snapshot trapped "Index out of range" (SIGTRAP, no `.ips`) — found only by re-measuring Clear all tasks inside the Tasks sheet with a plain-launch guard; the likely shape of the Back fault the push had. A fully custom bottom bar was built and REVERTED — never that bar (the home's bar is the chat's own input, not that). The Activity feed's unread tab badge must not return.
- **The home says what the list cannot (2026-09-23, the pass after the swap).** The day answer is COMPOSED, not the rank recited (`AI/DayAnswer.swift`, pure, `ComposedDayAnswerTests`): (1) **every row carries a reason** — one clause from the facts the rank read ("1 day overdue" · "Oldest of 4 decisions waiting · 21 days" · "Maya is waiting on this" · "Unblocks 2 more" · "Started, untouched 3 days" · "Next: Renew passport · 1 of 3 done" · "Nothing in the way · about 10 min"), rendered as the row's second line (`ChatMessage.reasons`, threaded through `InquiryAnswer`), so a row with no reason is a finding; (2) **time pressure seats ahead of judgment and decisions collapse to their oldest** — overdue, due today and urgent lead, ONE decision row stands for all of them, the rest follow in rank order; the LIST still puts Needs Decision on top and `TaskRanking` is untouched (the answer re-seats, never re-scores; precedent: the model-ranks reversal); (3) **an outcome speaks for its steps** (`Line.parentID`, a step whose umbrella is a candidate leaves; the umbrella's reason names the next step); (4) **the hour frames the lead** (`DayAnswer.Voice`: "deserve you first" before 18:00, "still deserve you" after, and an empty evening is "Nothing more needs you tonight." with nothing appended — a first-class quiet); (5) the openers are day answer → the person's OWN stall for started work the answer did not seat ("You started “X” 3 days ago and haven't touched it since.") → the evening's "Today, you finished …" minus anything the catch-up already names → the catch-up. **The glance strip opens the LIST, filtered, never a question** (`TasksPreset` through `\.openTasks`, which now takes one; `-TasksPreset overdue|dueToday|inProgress|waiting|decisions|done` reaches it): "2 overdue" and "What's overdue?" were one question twice, and "which ones?" is list vocabulary; "N open" is gone and the answer ends with **"and N more in Tasks"** (`DayAnswer.moreInTasks`, the catch-up's own "and N more in Activity" shape). The chips under a SEATED day answer are judgment questions only (`starterQuestions(for:seated:)`: the other caretaker's day, **"I've got 15 minutes"** — the `.quick` floor shape, unblocked work at or under `DayAnswer.quickMinutes` with the minutes as reasons — "What could I do while I wait?", the rot). **The AI's proposals live on the AI's surface:** `ParkedCapturesRow` and `GroupProposalRow` mount on the home above the day answer and NOT on the list, which is a plain workbench. **The home's rows carry their verb** (`ChatCitedTaskRow.gestures`, `showsVerbs` on the home only): the trailing slot is the recommended action as a small capsule running `performRecommended` with the same undo pill as the swipe, "Decide" on a decision OPENS the page (the detail's own carve-out), glyph-only at accessibility sizes (the word "Mark done" wrapped and squeezed the title), never the gradient; long-press offers **Hand to <name>** / That's mine through `claimAndLog` with an Undo pill — the home is where the household negotiates. **The empty home is about capture:** "Tell me everything on your mind." with one gradient CTA into the composer and `DataBoundary.captureShort` under it; the orb keeps its two placements. Judged on three new events (`glanceOpened(kind:)`, `homeRowActed(verb:)`, `tasksSheetDwell(elapsed:)` — the modal-depth meter: a sheet open for minutes is the home in exile).
- **The calm home (2026-09-23, the same evening, the owner's brief: parents, value fast, low cognitive load, space and pace).** The first composed home had thirteen numbers and nine kinds of element above the fold. Six kinds of element, three numbers. - **The home's design audit (2026-09-25, three hours by the owner's brief: space and pace, hierarchy, affordance, interaction, motion).** Measured on frame sheets from `simctl io recordVideo` tiled with ffmpeg, not on stills alone. What it changed: **(1) a re-seated opener keeps its id** (`InquiryStore.open`, `InquiryTests`), so the answer ANIMATES when the household moves — the done row fades, the next rises into the hero slot, "and 19 more" rolls to 18 (`contentTransition(.numericText)`) — where a fresh id had swapped the whole block; **(2) the ADVANCE on the hero's verb** (`ChatCitedTaskRow.completing`): the glyph fills, the row dims, the hand feels it, a `Motion.completeHold` beat, then the store moves — the list row's own choreography, reached by `-PressHomeVerb`; **(3) one card on the page**: quiet rows are containerless, the hero wears `Radius.card`, `Font.heroTitle` (22pt) and a control-size glyph; **(4) one accent**: "and N more in Tasks" is muted with a chevron, the hero's verb is the only blue; **(5) the launch settles** — rows one `Motion.staggerStep` apart, then the link, Ezra's questions and the news fade in after them (a rise as well doubled the text for a frame against the opener's entry transition — fade only); **(6) the way back to today**: a thread carries a leading "Today" button that clears and re-seats with the same settle, haptic, `homeReturned`; **(7) "Just added"**: what landed during THIS look (`DayAnswer.landed`, `lookStartedAt` reset on every foreground) and rank did not seat is listed under its own kicker with the answer's reasons and the list's arrival wash, and the thread scrolls it into view — a capture that only moved the count from 19 to 20 was not "seeing where it landed"; **(8) "Why this first?"** on the hero's long-press asks the household chat about that task; **(9) suggestions carry a micro arrow**, because muted text read as a caption; **(10) the kickers are headers** for VoiceOver. Not a finding: the ~1.3 s of black before the first frame is simulator process spawn (the process only starts logging 1.25 s after `simctl launch`), and the app is dark-only by design (`preferredColorScheme(.dark)`). `AccessibilityContractTests.homeControlsAreSpoken` pins the new controls. Also from the audit: **`-HomeHour N`** moves the home's clock (the evening voice and recap are otherwise unreachable on a simulator); **`captureLandedBelow(count:)`** meters how often a capture lands off the rows; the hero's title and the news line carry `.contentTransition(.identity)` (a crossfade drew each twice for two frames); every offset is guarded by `reduceMotion`; `HomeDesignContractTests` pins the invariants that are the ABSENCE of something — no verb on a quiet row, no container under it, no second accent, the kept opener id, the hold before the store moves. **The launch's first frames** (found on frame sheets, both fixed): a `FlowLayout` inside the bottom safe-area inset mis-measured on the first pass and drew its labels once more at the window origin (the suggestions are a plain stack now), and an implicit `.animation(value:)` on the bar's containers animated the bar's FIRST layout from a pre-layout frame — narrow, a third of the way up, sliding to the bottom over four frames — so the bar carries none; `HomeDesignContractTests.barHasNoImplicitAnimation` pins both. **"Why this first?" is a FLOOR shape** (`.whyFirst`, `DayAnswer.whyFirstAnswer`): asked of the model it answered "You do not know why …"; the ranking is the one thing the app knows exactly, so the answer is the hero's own reason and what follows it, with a lower row placed by number and a blocked one by what it waits on; the reason clauses become sentences through `DayAnswer.prose` ("it's the oldest of 3 decisions waiting and 10 days now"), never a caption glued into prose.
- **Size follows importance on the home (2026-09-25, the owner's brief: the bar and the suggestions are key).** The ranking, and the size each rank gets: (1) the hero task, `Font.heroTitle` 22pt, and its verb at `.ctaCompact` 15pt in a 36pt capsule (it had been a 13pt label, smaller than the reason under the title it acts on); (2) the ask field at `Font.composerInput` 17pt in a 52pt field (`LayoutMetrics.composerField`), the orb 52/34 and the send disc 36 beside and inside it; (3) the suggestions at `Font.suggestion` 15pt medium in primary text, each line a full 44pt tap target; (4) the quiet rows at 16pt; (5) the lead and the reasons; (6) the news and "and N more"; (7) kickers and the date; (8) the brand — **the title is INLINE on the home** (`.navigationBarTitleDisplayMode(.inline)`, the date still its subtitle), because the large title made "Ezra" the biggest text on the screen, 34pt over a 22pt hero, and spent ~100pt the answer and the bar needed. - **The deeper importance pass (2026-09-26, two hours: accessibility sizes, iPad, the smallest iPhone, first-run, motion frame by frame).** What it found and changed: **the launch stagger is a transition, never an `onAppear` opacity gate** — on iPad, a home first laid out under a presented sheet never flipped its flag and drew the lead and kicker with the hero and every row at zero opacity; a `sessionCommitted` flag now guards the park instead; **the task chat keeps the keyboard down while a reading is LOADING** (a regression the no-empty-band change caused: a loading reading stopped counting as an opener and the keyboard rose over it); **deck cards centre a one-line title** — the reserved second line was a blank subtitle, which pinned the title to the card's top beside a centred glyph; an invisible two-line twin now sets the height; the row's chip is still the area editor); **a disabled "Show me" mutes its label**; `-PressPrimary` fires once per process (a completion advanced the pager and the next page pressed its own CTA); **`-OpenSearch ["query"]`** reaches search, the one list surface no seam reached. Checked and left: the Settings reset card leads until dismissed by design; Manage Household's header; search itself. **Simulator trap:** `simctl spawn <udid> defaults write <bundle> …` writes the simulator's SHARED preferences, not the app container, so the value survives uninstall — a "fresh install" then skips onboarding; `defaults delete` it before judging a first run. **The home's catch-up crashed the app (found in this pass, fixed):** `refreshCatchUp` fetched `ChangeLogEntry` objects, read their strings into `@State`, and let the objects go; when Core Data unregistered them the strings pointed at freed snapshot storage and the next body pass died in `initializeWithCopy for InquiryAnswer` — 4 of 6 seeded launches crashed before the fix, 0 of 14 after. It now fetches `.dictionaryResultType` rows (no managed objects to tear down) and copies every string into native storage; **any read of the change log into view state must do the same.** Manage Household's header shrinks at accessibility sizes (56pt photo, `.sectionHeader` name) so the members are on the first screen. **The list's Undo was covered and clipped:** on the Tasks sheet the 62pt capture orb sat on the pill's trailing edge, and the pill's "Undo" truncated to "Un…" beside a long receipt — the only reversal for a completion. The sheet's pill now rides above the orb (`bottomInset` = orb + `Spacing.md`), and the Undo label is fixed-size with the message yielding; `-CompleteListRow N` reaches it (the reflow and pill are judged on a frame sheet). **The reveal's echo rests small at every size:** unfocused it fits its words (a one-line capture sat in an 88pt box) up to about three lines (a seven-item dump echoed five and left one and a half drafts above the fold), with a fade at its foot when clipped — the editor sets lines tighter than the oracle's `Text`, so a whole-line snap still sliced the last line; tapped, it gets its full editing room. **On a thread, "What deserves me today?" counts as asked** in the suggestions — the Today button already returns it. **Onboarding's two CTAs wrap inside their capsule** at accessibility sizes (inset, `minimumScaleFactor(0.8)`, `minHeight` not `height`); "Manage these for me" ran to the capsule's edges. **Onboarding seams act only while onboarding is on screen**: after any seed the app's container has `hasOnboarded`, so judge `-OnboardingIntro`/`-OnboardingResult` on a fresh install. **The Activity entry's detail offers the Undo it describes** (same `ChangeLogUndo.revert` write as the row), and its "Run: not recorded … this capture was committed before run tracking shipped" block appears only on rows that have a capture — it had been printed under archives, completions and hand-offs. `-OpenActivityDetail N` needs `-OpenActivity` beside it. **`-SeedGroupProposal`/`-AcceptGroupProposal` stay on the home** (they opened the Tasks sheet over the row they seed), and the row's seed hangs off a zero-size anchor — an empty `Group` never appears, so the seed never ran.

- **A product invariant that holds for the eyes and not for VoiceOver is broken (2026-09-20).** The app carries ~146 accessibility modifiers and no unlabelled glyph controls, so the gaps were operability, not labels — and the worst was a correctness break: "not yours to advance" removed the leading swipe and inerted the long-press menu on someone else's task while the row still offered **Complete** to VoiceOver. Four more, each its own lesson: **`.opacity(0)` hides from the eyes and NOT from the accessibility tree** — two height-reserving spacers were being read aloud, one of them the only warning about the 5s auto-submit, permanently true-sounding; **the leading swipe had no spoken equivalent** and these rows are a `LazyVStack`, not a `List`, so exposure was undocumented — `.accessibilityActions` is now declared beside the swipe, one owner for the gesture contract; **Undo dismissed on a 4s timer while its own announcement takes ~4s to speak**, so the app's only reversal was unreachable (`UndoNoticeView.dwell`, 14s under VoiceOver, sighted timing untouched); **the capture sheet opened into a LIVE MIC in silence** (hidden orb, no transcript by design, focus on the toolbar) and said nothing again until the reveal, up to 30s later on a cloud route — `announcePhase` speaks the two silent beats. Plus the selected ownership scope (`.isSelected`), the deck pager's named actions (the detail pager already had them), and a literal space smuggled in as a layout spacer being spoken verbatim. `AccessibilityContractTests` pins all of it by grep, because each property is the shape of a call site and no unit test can reach a SwiftUI accessibility tree.
- **Visible thinking has exactly two scales**: the full-screen `RambleOrb`, and `ThinkingMark`/`ThinkingLine` (one monochrome mark meaning "Ezra is thinking", in exactly two placements — a third, or one on an ambient wait, is the tripwire). - **Liquid Glass is a design system** (`Design/Glass.swift`): all glass through `.glassChrome`/`.glassCapsule`; glass is chrome never content; never carries primary text; neutral `.regular`; solid fallback under Reduce Transparency/Increase Contrast; `GlassEffectContainer` only at a composite site that morphs; `glassChrome` belongs in `.background`, never wrapped around foreground controls. Recede with `.recessed(_:)`, never a blur.
- **The grouping sweep PROPOSES, never writes** (`AI/GroupingSweep.swift`, 2026-09-17): the on-device model names which loose tasks serve one outcome (prefilter: shared word ≥4 letters, rejected sibling pairs cut first; judge behind `ModelRun`, capped; `validated(_:shown:)` rejects any judgment naming a title it was not shown, needs ≥2 members and a 1–6 word title, `childOf` tier 0.5); bare chains with no umbrella are candidates first (`chainClusters`), then — BUILT and OFF (`attachIsEnabled`, four configurations measured, none at FALSE ACCEPT 0; flip only over a clean `-GroupingSweepEval` report) — loose tasks beside an EXISTING outcome (`attachClusters` → "Add “X” to “Y”?", one `"linked"`/`parent` entry per member, directional `parentLink` suppression on no, tier 0.7), then word clusters — each task in one question; the sweep also runs 20 s after a successful `commit` (`runSoonAfterCommit`, ≤ once per 10 min); the output is a `GroupProposal` in memory and ONE quiet row on the HOME beside the parked-captures row (both moved off the list on 2026-09-23 — the AI's proposals belong on the AI's surface) (`GroupProposalRow`: Group them → the confirm card's `"grouped"` entry + Undo · Not these → `siblingGroup` suppressions + one reversible `"rejectedGroup"` entry · Not now → hidden this launch). Never a badge, never an edge without the person's tap. `-GroupingSweepEval` prints FALSE ACCEPT first; `-SeedGroupProposal` shows the row.

- **Every photo picker stays OUT of process — never pass `photoLibrary:` (2026-09-20).** That one argument is the whole difference between a picker needing no permission and one needing photo-library authorization, and the app declares no `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`, so an in-process picker TERMINATES it on open. All four carried it, including the one on the first screen a new user sees, under a doc comment that said the opposite. The simulator does not catch it (its permission state is usually primed). Every site only calls `loadTransferable` on the chosen item, so library access buys nothing. `SubmissionGateTests` greps it, comments stripped.
