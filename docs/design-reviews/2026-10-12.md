## Cross-cutting
This is a strong design system, applied with unusual discipline. No raw hex outside `Theme.swift`; `.system(size:)` appears only in avatar/marker glyph scaling; `glassEffect` is used only through `Glass.swift` and never under a material; no `ZStack` stands in for a sheet and no hand-rolled picker or alert exists; every icon-only button checked has an `accessibilityLabel`; destructive actions in capture, settings and parked captures confirm. Findings are therefore edge drift, not structural rot.

1. **One drift repeats across every flow: text-link controls below the app's own 44pt `LayoutMetrics.hitTarget`.** Activity inline Undo (`Features/Activity/ActivityView.swift:164-168`), roster Invite and relationship menu (`Features/Household/HouseholdRosterView.swift:295-298`, `:270`), onboarding links and ✕ (`Features/Onboarding/OnboardingView.swift:230,281,297,436`, `:697` is 32×32), Settings Privacy/Help links (`Features/Settings/SettingsView.swift:328-334`), advisor Decide/step rows (`Features/Components/AdvisorView.swift:424-428,458-486`), parked-captures row (`Features/Capture/ParkedCapturesRow.swift:120-181`), deck caption (`Features/Tasks/TaskDeckView.swift:173-202`), group proposal row (`Features/Tasks/GroupProposalRow.swift:155-177`), starter chips (`Features/Chat/ChatComponents.swift:571-589`). The rule exists and sibling controls obey it; the fix is mechanical. Severity: drift.
2. **`lineLimit(1)` on the person's own or the AI's words, against the app's own `relatedTitleLines` rule:** `Features/Components/AdvisorView.swift:504,527`, `Features/Capture/ConfirmCreationCard.swift:502,509,549,690,756,777`, `Features/Capture/ComposerView.swift:1504`, `Features/Tasks/TaskRow.swift:188`, `Features/Tasks/TaskDeckView.swift:148`, `Features/Tasks/GroupProposalRow.swift:162`. The merge chip (`ConfirmCreationCard.swift:502`) truncates the very title a destructive-ish decision names. Drift.
3. **Missing primitive: a related-task row.** Four hand-built variants in the detail (`Features/Detail/TaskDetailView.swift:905,961,1024,1190`) plus `TaskRow`, and a second pair, `ActivityRow` actor badge (`Features/Components/ActivityRow.swift:456-470`) vs `DetailActivityTimeline.dot` (`Features/Detail/DetailActivityTimeline.swift:132-146`). Also five copied capsule buttons in `ComposerView.swift:1534,1630,1916,2317,2440`.
4. **Dead views:** `TaskCardView` (`Features/Components/TaskCardView.swift:13`, only comments reference it) and `ChatDayKicker` (`Features/Chat/ChatComponents.swift:531`), and `chipContent` (`ConfirmCreationCard.swift:410`). Verified by grep. They carry stale comments and still cost the reader.
5. **Error states are thin everywhere writes happen.** `saveChanges()` results are ignored in list completion/cancel (`Features/Tasks/TaskLaneList.swift:74,90`), Activity undo (`ActivityView.swift:214`), Settings export (`try?`, `SettingsView.swift:124,569`). The detail shows a "Couldn't save" alert (`TaskDetailView.swift:448`), so the app disagrees with itself. No view has an offline state; that is correct for on-device capture and chat.
6. **Contrast (inferred from source):** `mutedText` (`Design/Theme.swift`) is reported at roughly 2.7:1 light and 3.5:1 dark by the capture reviewer's arithmetic, and carries 11-12pt copy: parked-row age, ownerReason/dueReason lines, "Why this?" toggle (`AdvisorView.swift:322-324`). The app is dark-only, so the dark figure is the live one. Re-measure before acting.
7. **Accessible name does not contain the visible label** (Voice Control): "Dictate" vs "Speak" (`ComposerView.swift:2456`), "Show category and effort" vs "Details" (`ConfirmCreationCard.swift:279`), "Your tasks" vs "Tasks" (`Features/Chat/HouseholdChatView.swift:173-183`). Drift.

## Per flow

### Shell and Ask home (`Features/Root/RootTabView.swift`, `Features/Chat/*`)
One NavigationStack rooted at the chat. Inline "Ezra" title with the date as subtitle; a lead sentence, one hero task card (22pt title, reason, one verb), up to three quiet rows, "and N more in Tasks", Ezra's questions, one news line; a pinned composer bar with the orb and suggestions. Tasks is a page-sized sheet.
- **Works well:** hierarchy is the clearest in the app. One accent, one card, size follows importance, and the empty home is about capture.
- Drift: starter chips are about 33pt (inferred) with no hit-target floor (`ChatComponents.swift:571-589`), while their siblings apply it (`:633,659-699`).
- Drift (thumb reach, inferred): the only control to the primary record, the Tasks list, is top-trailing (`HouseholdChatView.swift:173-183`). The in-content link at `:348` is absent when `moreInTasks` is nil.
- Polish: "Clear conversation" is `.destructive` and fires without confirm (`HouseholdChatView.swift:190-195`, `TaskAdvisorChatView.swift:138-142`). The thread is ephemeral, so low stakes. Free-form model replies render as one prose `Text` (`ChatComponents.swift:120-131`) when no task is cited.

### Task advisor chat (`Features/Advisor/*`, `Features/Components/AdvisorView.swift`)
Sheet titled by the task; the reading as the opener, a thread, follow-up chips, an "I've decided" alert. A failed reading renders nothing, by design.
- Drift: Decide buttons, "Pin to top", fallback links and `stepRow` (a 13pt single line that feeds `Correction` records) have no 44pt floor (`AdvisorView.swift:424-428,399-407,571-580,458-486`).
- Drift: `lineLimit(1)` on blocker and cited task titles (`:504,527`).
- Polish: every option's button is announced as just "Decide" (`:413-428`); observation and guidance are two stacked prose paragraphs (`:210-216`).

### Capture (`Features/Capture/*`) [churn]
Opens into listening (orb, status word, Done); typed captures land on a canvas; the reveal shows editable cards, a Group row, a "Left out" section and a pinned Create bar. Parking and Discard are confirmed.
- **Works well:** the reveal shows consequences, not classifier output; output is editable cards, not prose; the orb is never a spinner.
- Drift: the Create CTA is a fixed 52pt capsule whose label is generated from user text, `Create “<group>” · N steps · merge M` (`ComposerView.swift:1956-1967`, label at `:1978-2002`). At accessibility sizes it will clip the one label that says what pressing does (inferred).
- Drift: the parked-captures row, the only way back to unfinished words, is under 44pt and truncates to one line (`ParkedCapturesRow.swift:120-181`, `:156-161`).
- Drift: label-in-name and noun drift: Speak/Dictate/Say more; "3 things" vs "Create N tasks" (`ComposerView.swift:1470`); "Needs a decision" vs "Needs Decision" (`ConfirmCreationCard.swift:400`, `Features/Components/AssessmentChip.swift:67`).

### Tasks list (`Features/Tasks/*`) [churn]
Sheet over the home: ownership pills plus a three-axis filter, counts, status sections of rows (glyph, title, due, avatar). Swipe right acts, swipe left cancels, long-press menu; chains render as a horizontal deck; search is a separate sheet.
- Drift (trust): list completion and cancel post "Completed" plus Undo whether or not the save succeeded (`TaskLaneList.swift:74-75,90-92`; rows only haptic on failure, `TaskRow.swift:98,274,362`). Detail alerts on the same failure.
- Drift: ownership pills are 32pt with no `.minimumHitTarget()` (`TasksHomeView.swift:443-471`); deck caption and "N waiting" use `onTapGesture` on about 20pt text (`TaskDeckView.swift:173-202`), with accessibility traits added to compensate.
- Drift: search is a second, inconsistent list: it draws `TaskRowDivider` (the list has none), has no swipe actions, and mixes resolved with active (`Features/Tasks/TaskSearchView.swift:60-100`).

### Task detail (`Features/Detail/*`) [churn]
Full-screen pager. Custom back chevron, "n of N" counter, Ask and "…"; per page: kicker, editable title, a shape-driven spine, a property-chip card, a collapsed Details section, a pinned 52pt CTA.
- Drift (high): the "stop waiting on" × is a 12pt icon in `.pressableIcon`, which adds no hit area (`TaskDetailView.swift:1081-1089`, style at `Design/Motion.swift:260-275`). It performs a structural edit beside the title; the file's own comment at `:1857` admits mis-taps. Same class: date-picker Done (`:661-667`) and "Show all N" (`:1381`).
- Drift: four near-duplicate related-task rows (Cross-cutting 3).
- Drift: vocabulary for one concept: "Waiting on" / "Add wait" / "Stop waiting on", VoiceOver "Open blocker", trail "Added blocker" (`TaskDetailView.swift:860,1066,1089,1829,1842,1854`); "Up for grabs" vs "Shared / up for grabs" (`:748,755`).
- Drift (delegate vs manage): the property card always offers due, owner, status, urgent, estimate and wait, so unset ones show as "Not urgent", "No estimate" (`:636-646`): ranking inputs the person is asked to maintain.

### Activity (`Features/Activity/*`)
Day-grouped change feed in a sheet; each row has an avatar, a one-line byline, an inline Undo and a swipe Undo; tap pushes a detail page.
- Drift: the row opens its detail via `.onTapGesture` with no button trait or accessibility action (`ActivityView.swift:174`). The comment at `:145-148` explains the NavigationLink conflict; VoiceOver pays for it.
- Drift: the detail is an unguarded developer dump (Route, Rung, Hedge, Winning arm, Prompt tokens, Ungrounded drops, Autonomy, Model family/version) shown to customers (`ActivityDetailView.swift:129-380`, rationale in header `:7-13`). It conflicts with the guardrail that the customer never hears "AI" or a vendor name. Whether to gate it behind `#if DEBUG` or a disclosure is a product decision.
- Polish: inline Undo is 13pt with no frame while the detail's Undo has `minHeight` (`ActivityView.swift:164-168` vs `ActivityDetailView.swift:61`).

### Household and invites (`Features/Household/*`)
Pushed "Manage household": family header, one people card, per-member name, relationship menu, invitation caption, owner-only Invite and "…" menu; an invite sheet; an identity-link sheet on the joining phone.
- **Blocking:** "Remove from household" is `.destructive`, with no confirmation and no undo pill, directly under "Invite" in the same menu (`HouseholdRosterView.swift:310`). It is a soft delete (`deletedAt`, `:364`) but nothing in the UI restores the person (inferred from source).
- Drift: Invite button and relationship menu are below 44pt (`:295-298,270`).
- Drift: the identity sheet is `interactiveDismissDisabled` with no "not now" (`InviteViews.swift:199`). Probably intentional (identity gate); someone who joined the wrong household has only "Someone else".

### Onboarding (`Features/Onboarding/OnboardingView.swift`)
Dark-only four-phase flow: name, paste/screenshot/sample, settling, editable result with a "left out" section.
- Drift: row-remove ✕ is 32×32 (`:689-700`); secondary links lack a 44pt floor (`:230,281,297,436`). "Skip for now" is the only way past the name question.
- Drift: no back edge from result to the pasted text; a bad parse leaves "remove rows" or "Start empty", and the raw text is lost (inferred; comment at `:597`).
- Polish: "areas" in the copy, "Category, …" in the menu label (`:660`).

### Settings (`Features/Settings/SettingsView.swift`)
Sheet of cards: profile row, "Your week" digest toggle, "What leaves this device", Data (export, Clear all tasks, Reset everything), plus a reset receipt card.
- **Works well:** the page delegates; the AI engine card is gone and destructive actions confirm.
- Drift: "Privacy policy" and "Get help" links have no 44pt floor (`:328-334`); a failed export is silent (`try?`, `:124,569`).
- Polish: "Reset everything", the heaviest action, reads as a muted link by design (`:606-610,625-631`).

## Systems lens
The dominant strength is that the system is real and is policed: the rules files forbid raw colors and sizes, and `HomeDesignContractTests` pins the absence of things (no verb on a quiet row, no second accent). That is why the audit finds almost no color, spacing or glass drift. What it cannot find by grep is the next layer, and that is where the open work sits.

**Primitive or patch.** The missing concept is a *related-task row* (glyph slot, title with a two-line allowance, trailing meta, chevron, 44pt floor). Five hand-built versions across detail and list are why the 44pt floor and the two-line title rule are applied unevenly: each copy has to remember them. A second, smaller pair is the actor badge in `ActivityRow` and the detail timeline. A third is the capsule recipe repeated five times in the composer. The 44pt drift in Cross-cutting 1 is the same disease at a different scale: the floor lives in a constant, not in a text-link style, so every new text link re-decides it. A single `.textLink` button style with the floor built in would delete about ten findings.

**Simplicity is earned, mostly.** The home earns it: error, loading, empty and stop states all exist. The thin spot is write failure. Completion, cancel, Activity undo and export all assume the save worked, and the list tells the person it did. A clean surface with no failure branch is unfinished.

**Concept count.** The user meets: Ezra, Tasks, Activity, Household, Settings, Capture, Ramble (the CTA verb), Group, Split, Merge, Keep both, Left out, Details, Waiting on (also "blocker" in VoiceOver and the trail), Up for grabs, Needs a decision / Needs a plan, Decide, Why this first?. The undisciplined ones are "blocker" vs "waiting", "things" vs "tasks", "Speak/Dictate/Say more", and "area" vs "category". "Run", "Rung", "Hedge", "Winning arm" in Activity detail are data-model nouns that belong beneath the interface.

**Prominence implies supervision.** Handled well: visible thinking has exactly two scales, the home answers once instead of exposing the process, the grouping sweep is one quiet row. Counterexamples are the Activity detail machinery above, and the capture reveal's assumed-sparkle on up to six chips per card (inferred from the capture reviewer), which asks the person to supervise a read they just confirmed. A 0.7s artificial orb dwell over a ~2ms read (`Design/Motion.swift:180`, `ComposerView.swift:824-839`) is deliberate pacing, documented; keep an eye on it.

**Form is part of the answer.** Mostly right: capture, the home and the advisor return rows and cards. The exceptions are the advisor's observation/guidance prose and uncited free-form chat replies.

**Delegate or manage.** The task detail's always-present urgent/estimate/wait editors and the reveal's per-card eight editable fields are the main management surface; the code defends them as one tap each, not a form. Settings is the counterexample: two toggles, no engine controls.

**Layered complexity.** Good: Details is one collapsed disclosure, the home hides the strip. The Activity detail and the reset receipt are the two places depth is not layered.

**Making things true.** The home's essence is "what deserves me first, and one verb". Nothing on it is decoration. Tasks-as-a-modal-sheet carries modal-depth cost the code itself meters (`tasksSheetDwell`) and a doubled shell host (`RootTabView.swift:104,467-499`); that is a decided, measured tradeoff, so it is listed as a product watch item, not a finding. `RootTabView` is a stale name for a no-tab shell (polish, not user-visible).

## Top fixes
1. **Roster "Remove from household" without confirm or restore.** Add a confirmation dialog (or an Undo pill) — `Features/Household/HouseholdRosterView.swift:310`. **Blocking.**
2. **Tiny × on "stop waiting on".** Give it a 44pt hit area (`.minimumHitTarget()`), which also fixes `.pressableIcon` for the next user — `Features/Detail/TaskDetailView.swift:1081-1089`. Drift, high.
3. **List completion/cancel claims success without checking the save.** Check `saveChanges()` and post an error notice instead of "Completed" — `Features/Tasks/TaskLaneList.swift:74-75,90-92`. Drift, trust.
4. **Create CTA clips its generated label at large text.** Replace `.frame(height: 52)` with `minHeight: 52` and allow two lines — `Features/Capture/ComposerView.swift:1956-1967`. Drift.
5. **Build one text-link button style with the 44pt floor** and adopt it at the sites in Cross-cutting 1 — `ActivityView.swift:164`, `HouseholdRosterView.swift:295`, `OnboardingView.swift:230`, `SettingsView.swift:328`, `AdvisorView.swift:424`, `ParkedCapturesRow.swift:120`. Drift.
