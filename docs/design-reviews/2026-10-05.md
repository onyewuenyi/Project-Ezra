# Design audit, 2026-10-05

First audit; there is no previous one to carry forward. Everything about appearance below is **inferred from source**: nothing was built or rendered (cloud session, no Xcode). Severity: **blocking** / **drift** / **polish**.

## Scope
- Repo `onyewuenyi/project-ezra`, branch `main` at `ba89714` (PR #49). 187 Swift files, 40 files declaring a View, 54 View structs in `Features/`, 17.4k lines. No UIKit controllers.
- Read in full by flow: Ask home/shell, Tasks list, Capture, Detail + Advisor, Settings/Household/Onboarding/Activity/Digest, plus repo-wide greps with the surrounding code read for each hit. Six reviewers split the files; findings were spot-checked against source for the three blocking items.
- Commits since last audit: none to compare. Highest churn since 2026-09-21 (review-first): `ComposerView.swift` (11 touches), `TasksHomeView`, `GroupProposalRow`, `SettingsView`, `OnboardingView`, `CaptureFlow` (4 each).
- Skipped: Intents, AI/, Models, Debug diagnostics. Not reviewed as design surface.
- Inventory: tokens live in `Design/` (`Palette` adaptive hex, `Spacing`, `Radius`, scaled `Typography`, `LayoutMetrics` incl. `hitTarget` 44, `Motion`, `Glass`); shared components in `Features/Components`. `Assets.xcassets` holds only the app icon and an **empty** `AccentColor.colorset`; all colour is in code.

## Cross-cutting
1. **Text links never get a 44pt target (drift, systemic).** `.pressableLink` only dims on press (`Design/Motion.swift:266-274`). Every text-link call site inherits a sub-44 target: Undo `UndoNoticeView.swift:116-130`, Settings links `SettingsView.swift:326-336`, onboarding exits `OnboardingView.swift:230,436,514`, roster "Invite"/relationship menu `HouseholdRosterView.swift:266-300`, Activity Undo `ActivityView.swift:164-167`. One fix in the style removes the class. Similar: `PressableStyle` ignores `isEnabled`, so disabled buttons keep full gradient.
2. **Missing primitive: the capsule CTA.** A gradient capsule at fixed 52/54pt is hand-written at least 7 times (`ComposerView.swift:1116,1256,1964`, `InviteViews.swift:55`, `HouseholdChatView.swift:701`, `ChatComponents.swift:682`, `EmptyStateView.swift:45`), plus `OnboardingView.swift:219,425,621` and `TaskDetailView.swift:1463`. Fixed heights clip at accessibility sizes; only some sites got the `minHeight` fix. Related: a neutral capsule chip repeats ~10 times (`ChatComponents.swift:455,585,627,694`, `AdvisorView.swift:684`, `ActivityDetailView.swift:62`, `TasksHomeView.swift:653`, `ComposerView.swift:2448`, `UndoNoticeView.swift:135`) while the shared `MetadataChip.swift:42,55` goes unused by them. A 40pt bordered secondary button repeats at `ComposerView.swift:1543,1636,1921,2324,2447`.
3. **Dead code.** `TaskCardView.swift:13` (296 lines, own swipe gesture) is never instantiated; it drags `ConfidenceRow` and `AssessmentChip` with it, and docs still describe it as live. `ChatDayKicker` (`ChatComponents.swift:531`) has no references. Views reachable from nowhere are a finding: delete.
4. **Forced dark, half-built light.** `Project_EzraApp.swift:211` forces `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` (documented as a thesis choice), while `Palette.adaptive` ships light values (`Theme.swift:50-59`) that never render. Light-mode contrast bugs are unobservable. Decide: ship light, or delete the dead branch. Also onboarding repeats the override (`OnboardingView.swift:92`).
5. **Bare `onTapGesture` on real controls.** `TaskRow.swift:216`, `TaskDeckView.swift:179,202`, `ActivityView.swift:174`, `TaskCardView.swift:177` (dead). Row traits are patched in places, but there is no focus/pointer behaviour and no chevron on the Activity row. Orb tap `ComposerView.swift:1157` and keyboard dismiss `:330` are defensible.
6. **Hidden-only paths.** Completion on a plain row is swipe/long-press only (`TaskRow.swift:161-189,244`); "Hand to…" and "Why this first?" exist only in `.contextMenu` (`ChatComponents.swift:391,483-489`). Accessibility actions exist, sighted discoverability does not.
7. **State coverage.** Loading: only the Ask thread (`ChatComponents.swift:112`) and capture's orb; elsewhere local Core Data, fine. Error: failed saves in the task list produce a haptic and no message (`TaskRow.swift:98,274`); Settings export swallows its error (`SettingsView.swift:124`). Permission-denied: capture handles mic (`ComposerView.swift:2471-2497`); the Settings digest toggle does not (`SettingsView.swift:269-275`). Offline: not applicable to on-device flows, but the household invite relies on CloudKit and has only a mapped error (`HouseholdSharing.swift:55-58`).
8. **Motion bypass.** Six `withAnimation` calls skip `Motion` (`OnboardingView.swift:256,470,515,797,815,884`); Reduce Motion handling there is unconfirmed. Elsewhere Reduce Motion is thorough (18 sites).
9. **Fixed-size glyph fonts** that never scale: `ActivityRow.swift:167` (8pt), `DetailActivityTimeline.swift:138` (7pt). Decorative; drift against `Typography.swift:125-127`.
10. **Glass discipline is strong.** `.glassEffect` exists in `Glass.swift` only, applied at one site (`CaptureOrbButton.swift:61`), with Reduce Transparency/Increase Contrast fallbacks, no legacy materials. This is the opposite of the uniform-glass failure mode.

## Per flow

### Ask home (Chat, Root, Shell)
Opens on a composed "day answer": one hero task with a single verb, quiet rows, "and N more in Tasks", news line, parked/grouping rows (`HouseholdChatView.swift:316-448`). Bottom bar: ask field, 52pt capture orb, one suggestion (`ChatComponents.swift:778-884`). Tasks is a sheet from a top-trailing button; Activity/Household/Settings sit in a "…" menu.
1. **blocking** `UndoNoticeView.swift:116-130`: the Undo button is 13pt text, `.fixedSize()`, no frame or `minimumHitTarget`; the pill's padding (`:134`) is outside the Button, so the target is ~16-18pt tall. It is the sole reversal for completions, cancels, hand-offs and AI structural acts, and the pill dwells 4s (`:99`; 14s under VoiceOver). Give it a 44pt region and pause the timer while touched.
2. **drift** `HouseholdChatView.swift:200-204`, `ShellSurfaces.swift:46-50,81-89`: Activity, the supervision surface for what Ezra changed, is two taps deep in an overflow menu, unbadged by design; after an action the user sees a 4s pill and nothing durable. Give it a standing low-key entry (e.g. a "Review changes" line after an AI-structured commit).
3. **drift** `HouseholdChatView.swift:157-183`: Tasks, the record of the user's own work, is a text button top-trailing (hardest reach) while ask and capture sit at the bottom; the in-thread link `:348` only appears on overflow. Always show the in-thread link or bring it within thumb reach.
Also: `EmptyStateView.swift:38-48` CTA is ~42pt tall with no `minHeight` (the home's own CTA at `HouseholdChatView.swift:700` has one). "Clear conversation" has no confirm (`:190-195`, polish). ThinkingMark "exactly two placements" rule has drifted (`ThinkingMark.swift:20-24` vs `ChatComponents.swift:113`, `InviteViews.swift:80`).
Works: structured, one-verb answer instead of prose; every icon-only control labelled; Dynamic Type via real tokens.

### Tasks list
"My/Our Tasks" sheet: Done top-left, search and "…" top-right (`TasksHomeView.swift:212-248`); scope pills (only with ≥2 members) and filter capsule; count strip; status-grouped `TaskRow`s with swipe actions; dependency work as a horizontally paged deck.
1. **drift** `TasksHomeView.swift:459-470`: scope pills are `minHeight: 32` with zero horizontal padding when unselected, 12pt apart; the sibling filter capsule gets `.minimumHitTarget()` (`:626`). A native segmented `Picker` does what the custom matched-geometry pill does (`:484` records why glass was avoided, not why not native).
2. **drift** `TaskDeckView.swift:142-202`: "N waiting" is a bare 11pt Text with `onTapGesture` nested inside the caption's own `onTapGesture`; the caption is the only way "up" to the umbrella task and is ~16pt tall. Make it a 44pt Button.
3. **drift** `TaskRow.swift:162-171` vs `TaskDeckView.swift:298`: the same status glyph is inert on a row (complete via hidden swipe/long-press) but a Menu in the deck. Make completion a visible 44pt control everywhere.
Also: three independent filter axes plus ~11 nouns on one sheet; "Done" is both the dismiss button (`:213`) and a status (concept collision); `lineLimit(1)` on user titles below accessibility sizes (`TaskRow.swift:177`, `LayoutMetrics.swift:40`) clips long titles at default size.
Works: accessibility equivalents for every swipe; solid, not glass, for text-bearing selection; four tailored empty states; native `.searchable`.

### Capture
Opens into a full-screen mic orb; 5s silence or Done goes to understanding (orb takeover ~0.7s deterministic, longer if the judge runs); then one scrolling reveal: transcript echo, mic/photo/Re-read row, editable cards, group button, "Left out", pinned Create (`ComposerView.swift:1408-1424`).
1. **blocking (failure path)** `ComposerView.swift:1970`, `:577-579`: Create is disabled while `commitPendingRetry`; the alert's "Keep Editing" leaves the flag set, so after dismissing there is no way to save except Close/Discard. The disabled Create also looks live because `PressableStyle` ignores `isEnabled` (`Motion.swift:260-275`), the same dead-affordance fault removed at `:338-342`. Relabel as "Try saving again" and enable, or drop "Keep Editing".
2. **drift** `ComposerView.swift:1959-1964,1988-2000`: Create is fixed 52pt with an unbounded label ("Create "Thanksgiving" · 5 steps · merge 1"); same class at 1542, 1635, 1920, 2324, 2447. Use `minHeight`, `lineLimit(2)`, `minimumScaleFactor(0.8)` like onboarding.
3. **drift** `ComposerView.swift:1408-1424`: three non-answer elements sit above the first card; the 44pt "say more" row asks for work before the person has judged the result. Move mic/photo beside "Left out"; keep Re-read only when edited.
Also: Voice Control label mismatch, visible "Speak"/"Say more", `accessibilityLabel("Dictate")` (`:2456`); `sparkle` means Ramble, Re-read and "AI assumed" (`:1110,1916`, `ConfirmCreationCard.swift:850`); orb vs instant reveal depends on whether the judge fires (`CaptureFlow.swift:69-85`), so prominence is unpredictable; auto-submit is announced only in the last 2s at 12pt (`:1340-1358`); When?/Urgent chips use `.plain` and lose press feedback (`ConfirmCreationCard.swift:335,807`).
Works: the orb is a quiet signal that carries no interpretation; reveal is structurally final (`Interpretation.swift`); output is cards, not prose; Discard is confirmed, card removal has Undo, Close parks; manage controls appear only on evidence.

## Systems lens
**Primitive or patch.** The system is real, which makes its gaps visible. Colour, type, spacing, radius and motion all come through tokens (62 Feature files use `Palette`; only ~13 literal colour hits, mostly the orb's art in `RambleOrb.swift`; zero literal corner radii; no blanket Dynamic Type clamps). The patch pattern is in the interaction layer, not the visual one: the capsule CTA is rewritten per screen at 40/52/54pt, the capsule chip ten times beside an unused shared chip, and the 44pt rule is a convention applied by hand rather than baked into the link/button styles, which is why the same under-target bug recurs in every flow. Fix the style once.
**Making things true / delete.** The essence is "one hero task, one verb, reversible acts". Delete `TaskCardView` and its two satellites, `ChatDayKicker`, and the unreachable light palette unless light mode is a decision. The deck, scope pills and three filter axes spend prominence on the list the product deliberately demoted to a sheet.
**Concept count.** Home alone exposes day answer, hero, Tasks, "Just added", parked capture, group proposal, news, Activity, Household, Settings, ask, capture. The Tasks sheet adds status, attention, category, scope, group/outcome/deck, step, blocker/waiting, urgent, decision. "Done" means dismiss and status. Activity leaks Rung/Engine/Model family, which are data-model nouns that belong beneath the interface.
**Prominence implies supervision.** Mostly well judged: capture's orb carries no interpretation, the reveal is final, Advisor suggests and the human accepts. Two lapses: capture's orb takeover is unpredictable (judge arm decides), and the only durable account of what Ezra changed is a menu item plus a 4s pill. Quiet signal and full takeover exist; the middle layer (an optional standing preview of changes) is missing.
**Form.** Right shape throughout: ranked rows with one-clause reasons, cards with consequence-only lines, structured advisor options. No prose-where-structure-belongs found.
**Delegate or manage.** Settings is exemplary (two toggles). The management creep is in Tasks (three filter axes, scope pills) and capture's reveal (Re-read, Split, Group, mic, photo around the answer), though capture gates most of them on evidence.
**Layered complexity.** Detail is the largest top-level stack (spine, dependents, advisor, chips, description, details, bar line) but collapses Details well.
